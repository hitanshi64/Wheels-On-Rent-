About WheelsOnRent

WheelsOnRent is a vehicle rental platform built for India, offering everything from bicycles and Activas to bikes and cars — all on one easy-to-use website.

Our Mission

Make renting a vehicle as simple as a few clicks — affordable, transparent, and available across major Indian cities.

Wide Fleet

Choose from cycles, scooters/Activas, motorcycles, and cars depending on your trip and budget.

Safe & Verified

We collect basic renter and licence details before every booking to keep our fleet and community safe.

How It Works

  1. Create a free account with your name, email, and phone number.
  2. Browse available bikes, cycles, cars, and Activas near you.
  3. Pick your dates, share your licence details, and confirm your booking.
  4. Pay online or at pickup, then enjoy the ride!
